# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Players of Danger Ghost, including Web3 and DeSo blockchain enthusiasts. They want a seamless, engaging, and immersive browser-based RPG experience where they can progression-save their characters, manage inventories, and gear up without breaking immersion. This also includes players who need accessibility considerations like dyslexia-friendly layouts.

## Product Purpose
A 33-level Web3 RPG game integrated with the DeSo blockchain. The goal of this version (v2.0) is to transition from an external HTML control shell to an immersive, full-screen, in-canvas overlay system, allowing players to feel fully engaged in the gameplay.

## Brand Personality
- Cyberpunk
- Immersive
- High-contrast neon

## Anti-references
- Clunky external HTML control menus outside the canvas.
- Low-contrast, hard-to-read text or tiny font sizes (less than 16px).
- Saturated generic SaaS-style cards, diagonals, or stripes used as background decorations.
- Overly rounded borders (keep cards around 12–16px border-radius).

## Design Principles
1. **Total Canvas Immersion:** The viewport is the game. No scrolling out of the game canvas or interacting with external HTML shells.
2. **Glassmorphism Overlays:** Semi-transparent menus (`rgba(24, 18, 36, 0.75)` with backdrop blur) that hover over the gameplay loop to show character status and inventory.
3. **Accessibility First (Dyslexia-Friendly):** High readability font stacks (like Inter) with safe sizes (16px base, 24px headings) and 1.5 line heights.
4. **Neon Accents:** Glow, light, and border styling using cyberpunk greens, cyans, magentas, and gold.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Dyslexia-friendly typography (Inter / Roboto, size >=16px, line-height 1.5).
- High visual contrast (minimum 4.5:1 ratio).
- Reduced motion alternatives for CSS animations (transcription/opacity fades instead of moving animations).
