The Origins of Nowhere

Welcome to the remnants of the digital realm. After the great collapse of the centralized web, only fragmented souls known as "Ghosts" survived in the blockchain.

You play as a rogue Ghost navigating the dark corridors of the "Nowhere" servers, hunting for lost fragments of data, ancient weapons, and spells to defeat the corrupted entities that rule this wasteland.

Relics and Evolution

Every piece of gear you find is cryptographically bound to your essence. When you save your evolution on the DeSo blockchain, your Ghost becomes stronger permanently, crossing dimensions and surviving system reboots.
