Danger Ghost Quick Start Guide

Follow these 4 simple steps to begin your journey in Nowhere:

  1. Connect Your Wallet: Click the CONNECT DESO WALLET button on the start screen. This generates your permanent account link.
  2. Select Your Character: Choose your Ghost NFT from the list, or mint your first one 100% free (only pay tiny network cents) to begin with Level 1 stats.
  3. Allocate Stat Points: Collect score in game to level up. When you do, press K to open the RPG drawer and allocate attributes (VIT, AGI, INT, POW, MAG).
  4. Upgrade Weapon: Check your weapon stats in the status drawer. If you collect enough points, click the gold upgrade button to increase your attack power.
💡 Pro Tip: Hold T in-game to speed up the simulation speed (2x physics) for fast-paced dungeon clearance!

THE LORE OF FTASMA

In the depths of Nowhere, souls wander stripped of flesh. The protagonist of the saga is Ftasma, an incorporeal spectral projection born directly as a ghost in Niterói in the year 2012. His past history — how he became this specter — remains hidden under a veil of absolute mystery to the entire cosmos and to himself.

Instead of being paralyzed, Ftasma uses his cosmic amnesia as strength. Immune to spiritual manipulation because he possesses no true name, he acts as a wise and opportunistic anti-hero, manipulating the laws of the rift to gain advantages and increase his power at all costs.

In the year 2020, to avoid his cosmic dissolution, he performed the Spiritual Migration, integrating his soul directly into the DeSo blockchain. He converted his consciousness into elliptic curve keys and immutable block records, ensuring his digital immortality.

Cryptographic Persistence

Ftasma's soul does not reside on fragile local servers. Each level milestone, items in the BAG, and RPG attributes are compiled into a cryptographic Base64 sheet and recorded on-chain in the DangerGhost_SaveState metadata.

At the end of his 33-level campaign, the hero's soul can be minted permanently as a spectral NFT, securing his immortality on the eternal ledger and allowing other hunters to invoke his power.
