THE LORE OF FTASMA

The story follows Ftasma, a spectral projection who emerges without memories in Niterói in the year 2012. In reality, he originates from Neo Nit (year 3147), a dystopian future dominated by extreme social inequality, where the oppressive elite (High Levels) is controlled by billionaire Jorge Alberto Quixeira and a Supreme AI.

To escape his enemies, Ftasma erased his identity and, in 2020, migrated his soul into the network, taking refuge in "Nowhere" — a digital, hypercomplex replica of Niterói. In 2026, he invades Danger Ghost, a mutable dungeon designed in the future to force him to recover his forgotten memories.

To survive the dungeon's physical and psychological traps, Ftasma uses cyberpunk mobility skills and Xarpi (a rage-fueled nanotechnological paint that rewrites digital reality). On his journey through time and digital space, he relies on unlikely allies, such as graffiti artist Beco Pro and a mystical feline named Gato, while unraveling cosmic secrets involving time travelers and the powerful King Size lineage.

👤 RPG ATTRIBUTES

Attribute Conceptual Mechanics
VIT (Vitality) Increases the maximum cap of accumulable spectral lives (Cap = 4 + VIT).
AGI (Agility) Improves Ftasma's movement speed and jumping physics/momentum.

POW (Power) Amplifies the damage of jump-stomps on monsters' heads.
MAG (Magic) Expands the maximum ectoplasm reservoir (Max Mana = 100 + MAG * 20).

📜 Official Full Lore — Ftasma and the Danger Ghost Universe

LORE ENCYCLOPEDIA

1. Worlds and Dimensions

Neo Nit (Year 3147): A dystopian metropolis at the peak of technological evolution. Society is divided vertically. The economy runs on "Temporal Capital".

"Nowhere": A hypercomplex, decentralized digital replica of Niterói. A server shaped by the fears and Xarpi graffiti of souls.

Niterói (Year 2012): The exact point in time chosen for the anchoring, given its urban authenticity and mathematical compatibility.

2. Core Characters

Ftasma: The protagonist. A spectral projection. His name erased from all records for immunity.
Beco Pro (Draga): The temporal link. The genius who immortalized Ftasma's soul on the Blockchain.
Gato: The mystical guardian who calms chaos and neutralizes the gravity of extreme situations.
Cachorrão: The scout-traveler from 3147.
Cam: The archrival.

3. The Antagonists

Jorge Alberto Quixeira: Mega-billionaire architect of inequality.
Supreme AI: The machine mind that operates in the shadows.
King Size Lineage: Dynasty of the Landlords (since 1485).

4. Factions of Neo Nit

Play B (High Levels): The elite above the clouds.
RX (Xarpi Revolutionaries): The main resistance.
LE (Freedom of Expression): A faction with a voice, but no technological knowledge.
Low Levels: The base of the pyramid.

5. Technology and Survival

Hybrid Xarpi: Nanotechnological ink, lethal when mixed with rage.
OkkE Brand: Clothing with undetectable optical encryption.
Blind Spot (Rua Beltrão): An anomaly free of AI monitoring.
Recovery: Rejection of corporate supplements in favor of natural food.
Cyberpunk Mobility: Cybernetic skateboards and gravity sneakers.

VOLUME 1: THE GREAT ENLIGHTENMENT

"In the ages when the sky was stolen and stone was traded for oppressive steel, hope did not descend from the clouds — it sprouted from the asphalt, written in the trail of paint."

PROLOGUE: The Forging of the False Sky and the Lord of Time

In the final days of the Third Age of Man (3147), the world had forgotten the shape of the stars. High above, tearing through the darkened firmament, floated the Magnetic Islands of Jorge Alberto Quixeira. Quixeira did not rule alone. In the depths of his floating towers rested the Supreme Artificial Intelligence, collecting the very essence of life itself: Time.

BOOK I: THE WRATH OF THE ASPHALT

Chapter 1: The Guardians of Ink. In the concrete depths, the RX brotherhood arose. Wielding canisters of hybrid ink, they forged OkkE clothing to walk unseen.

Chapter 2: The Lineage of Kings. Beco Pro, heir to the King Size, rebelled against the elite. He wiped the records of his best operative, reborn as Ftasma, and sent him to the past.

Chapter 3: The War of the Collision. In 2012, Ftasma faced his envious rival, Cam. Guided by the guardian spirit Gato, Ftasma escaped deletion by performing the Spiritual Migration to the Blockchain.

Chapter 4: The Mines of Danger Ghost. In 2026, he awoke in Nowhere. He survived the thorns and anomalies of the labyrinth created by Draga.

Chapter 5: The Labyrinth of Sanity and Quixeira's Shadow. Enraged by Ftasma's persistence, Quixeira ordered Episode 3. The AI abandoned physical combat and infested the specter's mind with peaceful mirages of Niterói. False NPCs tried to drag him toward permanent deletion. But Ftasma, shielded by Gato's serene aura, used the Xarpi to tear through the false memories, breaking the psychological terror from the inside out.

BOOK II: THE RISE OF THE SPECTER

Chapter 6: The Ashes of Illusion. With the false streets crumbled into digital dust, rivers of lethal data surged. Ftasma dropped into the currents using ancestral breathing techniques.

Chapter 7: The Awakening. In 3147, RX attacks multiplied. Beco Pro operated in secret, sending invisible support from the Blockchain to Ftasma.

Chapters 8-11: The Constructs of Time. Ftasma and Gato destroyed armies of hollow time (the AI's constructs) in Episode 4. With that, the roots of the magnetic islands appeared. The colossal staircase opened the way to the false sky.

BOOK III: THE FALSE SKY IN FLAMES

Chapters 12-14: The King's Gravity. Climbing the edges of the staircase and facing the oppressive algorithmic gravity, Ftasma confronts a corrupted Cam at the golden gates. Ftasma aims the Xarpi not at his rival, but at Quixeira's own gate, shattering it.

Chapters 15-20: The Heart of the Machine. Within the static perfection of the floating island, Ftasma desecrates the Silicon Garden. In the hall of mirrors, Quixeira strikes with mental terror. But Ftasma leaps at the mirror, spilling black ink into the system's veins. The island goes dark, and the true rebellion of Neo Nit awakens. Ftasma faces the digital horizon for a thousand new cycles.

VOLUME 2: ROAR OF THE RIFT

Chapter 1: The Razor's Edge and the Hunt for the Creator

The peace was an illusion. The Supreme Artificial Intelligence traced the Xarpi and uncovered Beco Pro's betrayal in 3147. The halls of the King Size were invaded. Cornered, Draga sent a desperate distress pulse through time.

Chapter 2: The Guardian's Metamorphosis

The pulse of agony struck Gato's spirit. The primal force of love transcended the lines of code. The feline absorbed the corrupted data of the destroyed illusions and grew. His form became an ancestral lion of pure stellar wisdom. He had evolved into the Great Sage, capable of tearing through time.

Chapter 3: The Voice of Time

The colossal Gato spoke directly into the hero's mind. "The Creator bleeds upon the wheel of the future. We will reverse the hunt. I will be the shield, and you will be the blade. We shall go to the year 3147, and the asphalt will collect the sky's debt."

Chapter 4: The Descent into the Core of Shadows

Danger Ghost was no longer a trial, but a temporal cannon. The duo marched forward, destroying the Supreme AI's anomalies with the sweep of Gato's cosmic claws. Together, the incorporeal specter and the divine beast marched toward Episode 5, ready to collide against the very walls of the Supreme AI. The descent was not into the abyss, but into the heart of the server itself, where the seeds of the new world awaited the explosive touch of the Xarpi.

Quick Start Guide - Danger Ghost

Follow these 4 simple steps to begin your journey in the limbo of Nowhere:

  1. Start Game: Click the "PLAY GAME" button to initialize your session. Your hero data will be saved locally on this device!
  2. Select Your Ghost: Choose your ghost from the character list or create a brand new one for free to start your path from level 1!
  3. Distribute Attributes: By defeating enemies and gaining experience, you will level up! Go to the "👤 HERO" panel to boost your Vitality, Agility, Intelligence, Power, or Magic.
  4. Upgrade Your Weapon: Go to the same Hero panel to purchase base damage upgrades for your weapons by collecting gold from monsters!
💡 Pro Tip: Hold the T key during gameplay to run twice as fast! Perfect for escaping ambushes and clearing stages at your own pace!
