THE LORE OF FTASMA

In the depths of Nowhere, souls wander stripped of flesh. The protagonist of the saga is Ftasma, an incorporeal spectral projection born directly as a ghost in Niterói in the year 2012. His past history — how he became this specter — remains hidden under a veil of absolute mystery to the entire cosmos and to himself.

Instead of being paralyzed, Ftasma uses his cosmic amnesia as strength. Immune to spiritual manipulation because he possesses no true name, he acts as a wise and opportunistic anti-hero, manipulating the laws of the rift to gain advantages and increase his power at all costs.

In the year 2020, to avoid his cosmic dissolution, he performed the Spiritual Migration, integrating his soul directly into the digital universe. He converted his consciousness into secure local data structures, ensuring his digital immortality.

Cryptographic Persistence

Ftasma's soul does not reside on fragile local servers. Each level milestone, items in the BAG, and RPG attributes are compiled into a cryptographic Base64 sheet and recorded on-chain in the DangerGhost_SaveState metadata.

At the end of his 33-level campaign, the hero's soul can be minted permanently as a spectral NFT, securing his immortality on the eternal ledger and allowing other hunters to invoke his power.

👤 RPG ATTRIBUTES

Attribute Conceptual Mechanics
VIT (Vitality) Increases the maximum cap of accumulable spectral lives (Cap = 4 + VIT).
AGI (Agility) Improves Ftasma's movement speed and jumping physics/momentum.
INT (Intelligence) Speeds up mana regeneration rate and optimizes Ghost Mode mana consumption.
POW (Power) Amplifies the damage of jump-stomps on monsters' heads.
MAG (Magic) Expands the maximum ectoplasm reservoir (Max Mana = 100 + MAG * 20).

Quick Start Guide - Danger Ghost

Follow these 4 simple steps to begin your journey in the limbo of Nowhere:

  1. Start Game: Click the "PLAY GAME" button to initialize your session. Your hero data will be saved locally on this device!
  2. Select Your Ghost: Choose your ghost from the character list or create a brand new one for free to start your path from level 1!
  3. Distribute Attributes: By defeating enemies and gaining experience, you will level up! Go to the "👤 HERO" panel to boost your Vitality, Agility, Intelligence, Power, or Magic.
  4. Upgrade Your Weapon: Go to the same Hero panel to purchase base damage upgrades for your weapons by collecting gold from monsters!
💡 Pro Tip: Hold the T key during gameplay to run twice as fast! Perfect for escaping ambushes and clearing stages at your own pace!
